About us

Trusted export partner from Kenya

Arriana Exports Ltd specialises in the export of macadamia nuts, cashew nuts, edible oils, halal meats, vegetables, beans & legumes, tubers and fruits.

We are a team of dedicated professionals based at Spur Mall, First Floor unit F60, Ruiru, Kiambu County. With years of experience, we have established ourselves as a trusted supplier of high-quality food and nuts produce to customers around the world.
